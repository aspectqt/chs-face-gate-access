Quick Start

Follow this order for a smooth daily workflow.

1. Prepare students and sections

Use the Students module to verify profiles, year levels, sections, and face registration details.

2. Start scanning from Dashboard

Confirm the scan mode first, then begin scanning when the camera area is ready for real-time multi-face detection and recognition.

3. Watch live activity and verification

Admins can open Live Gate Monitoring while staff continue real-time multi-face scanning.

4. Validate logs and reports

Check Gate Logs, SMS Logs, and Analytics after scanning when you need confirmation or troubleshooting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common answers for everyday issues.

How do I start scanning correctly?
Open Dashboard, confirm the current scan mode, then begin scanning when the camera area is ready. The system supports real-time multi-face detection and recognition, while clear lighting and unobstructed faces help maintain accuracy.
Why is a student not being recognized?
Check lighting, camera framing, and whether the student's face is clearly visible. The system can detect multiple faces in real time, but blur, occlusion, and poor positioning can still reduce recognition accuracy. Then verify the student profile and face registration in the Students module.
Where should I confirm attendance results?
Start with Gate Logs to review the exact action, time, and session. Check SMS Logs next if you also need to verify notification behavior.
When should an admin open Live Gate Monitoring?
Open it during active scanning whenever you need live observation of recognition activity, feed updates, and real-time monitoring status.
